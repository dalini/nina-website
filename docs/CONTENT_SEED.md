## Bio (facts to confirm)
- Born 1977 in Hilden; lives and works in Karlsruhe.
- 2010–2017 Staatliche Akademie der Bildenden Künste Karlsruhe (Silvia Bächli, John Bock, Ulla von Brandenburg); Meisterschülerin of Harald Klingelhöller.
- Represented by galerie burster (Berlin / Karlsruhe).

## Awards and grants
- 2019 Fellowship, Cité internationale des arts, Paris
- 2018 Hanna Nagel Preis, Karlsruhe
- 2017 Prize of the 23rd Karlsruhe Artists' Fair
- 2013 Academy prize, winter exhibition, AdBK Karlsruhe
