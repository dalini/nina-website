## Requirement

- **Statement**: The CV page MUST show short bio, education, solo exhibitions, works in public space, group exhibitions, and awards and grants, each as its own section containing the published CV entries of that section.
- **Rationale**: Curators and press read the CV by category (FR-5.2).
- **Assumptions**: None
- **Out of Scope**: Deriving the exhibition and public-space sections from Exhibition entries and works, hiding and overriding derived lines (FR-5.3, FR-5.4); ordering within sections (FR-5.2: newest year first); Downloads on the CV page (FR-6.1). Decided in step 10 and decomposed in the step 9 re-run.

- **Architecture Traceability**: Content Store (CV entry), Site Build

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **AC-01 — Expected behavior**: Given published CV entries in all six sections, when the Site Build runs, then the CV page shows six sections in the order of FR-5.2, each with a heading and its entries in the page's language.
2. **AC-02 — Boundary or failure behavior**: Given a section with no published entry, when the CV page is generated, then the page renders without errors and that section, including its heading, is not shown (FR-5.2).
3. **AC-03 — Prohibited behavior**: Given an unpublished CV entry, when the CV page is generated, then it does not appear.
