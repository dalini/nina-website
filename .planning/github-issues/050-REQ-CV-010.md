## Requirement

- **Statement**: The CV page MUST show the sections short bio, education, solo exhibitions, works in public space, group exhibitions, and awards and grants in this order with the DESIGN.md section labels, hide sections without entries, and list the CV entries in each section newest year first and entries of the same year alphabetically by their wording, with the short bio (no year) first.
- **Rationale**: FR-5.2 fixes the CV structure; the bio, education and awards are entered as CV entries (FR-5.4).
- **Assumptions**: None
- **Out of Scope**: Derived lines (REQ-CV-020); hiding and overriding them (REQ-CV-030); downloads (REQ-DOWNLOADS-010).

- **Architecture Traceability**: Site Build

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **AC-01 — Expected behavior**: Given CV entries in bio, education (2008, 2012) and awards (2018), when the CV page is generated, then the sections appear bio, education, awards, with 2012 before 2008.
2. **AC-02 — Boundary or failure behavior**: Given no entries in any exhibition or public-space section, when the CV page is generated, then those section headings do not appear.
3. **AC-03 — Boundary or failure behavior**: Given a CV-only line in solo exhibitions for 2009, when the CV page is generated, then the solo exhibitions section shows it.
4. **AC-04 — Prohibited behavior**: Given an unpublished CV entry, when the CV page is generated, then it does not appear.
5. **AC-05 — Additional criterion**: Given two education entries of 2010 reading `Universität der Künste Berlin` and `Akademie der Bildenden Künste Karlsruhe`, when the CV page is generated, then the Akademie line is listed first.
