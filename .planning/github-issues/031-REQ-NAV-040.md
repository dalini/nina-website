## Requirement

- **Statement**: The Site Build MUST provide one shared date format used by every page that shows a date: German pages show `DD.MM.YYYY` (e.g. "12.03.2026") and English pages `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g. "2026-03-12"), always with two-digit day and month; a date range shows both full dates separated by an en dash with a space on each side ("12.03.2026 – 03.05.2026", "2026-03-12 – 2026-05-03"); this applies to Exhibition dates on the list and on the entry pages, News dates and the date in the Instagram fallback alt text.
- **Rationale**: One format in both languages avoids ambiguous day/month orders for international visitors (FR-1.8).
- **Assumptions**: None
- **Out of Scope**: Which dates each page shows (REQ-EXHIBITIONS-010, REQ-EXHIBITIONS-030, REQ-NEWS-010, REQ-INSTAGRAM-020); the year-only values of works, Texts and CV lines, which are not dates (FR-3.2, FR-5.1, FR-5.3).
- **Design Traceability**: DESIGN.md Components (Interface labels: Dates, DE `DD.MM.YYYY`, EN `YYYY-MM-DD`, ranges); `style-guide.html` as the rendered reference
- **Architecture Traceability**: Site Build (generates the DE and EN pages)

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **AC-01 — Expected behavior**: Given the date 2026-03-12, when it is formatted for a German page and for an English page, then the results are `12.03.2026` and `2026-03-12`.
2. **AC-02 — Expected behavior**: Given the range 2026-03-12 to 2026-05-03, when it is formatted, then the German result is `12.03.2026 – 03.05.2026` and the English `2026-03-12 – 2026-05-03`, with an en dash (U+2013) and one space on each side.
3. **AC-03 — Boundary or failure behavior**: Given the dates 2026-01-05 and 2026-12-31, and a range that starts and ends on the same day, when they are formatted, then day and month always have two digits (`05.01.2026`, `2026-01-05`), and the same-day range shows the date twice as a range like any other.
4. **AC-04 — Prohibited behavior**: Given the built German and English pages of fixture content with Exhibition entries, News items and an Instagram post without caption, when they are searched, then no date appears in another format (no month names, no `MM/DD/YYYY`, no time of day), and every date shown comes from the shared format.
