## Requirement

- **Statement**: The Site Build MUST add to the CV a line for every published past and current Exhibition entry, of type solo or two-person under solo exhibitions and of type group or fair under group exhibitions, and a line for every published work marked in public space under works in public space; upcoming entries appear once they open; derived lines MUST be merged with CV-only lines by year. A line derived from an Exhibition entry MUST read year, title, venue, city (e.g. "2025  Faltungen, galerie burster, Karlsruhe"), with the entry's start year.
- **Rationale**: Nina enters each exhibition once (FR-5.3).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **AC-01 — Expected behavior**: Given a past solo entry 2019, a current two-person entry 2026, a past fair entry 2021 and a published public-space work 2020, when the CV is generated, then the first two are under solo, the fair under group and the work under works in public space.
2. **AC-02 — Boundary or failure behavior**: Given an upcoming group entry, when the CV is generated before it opens, then it is absent; when generated on its start date, it is present.
3. **AC-03 — Boundary or failure behavior**: Given a CV-only line 2020 and a derived line 2021 in group exhibitions, when the CV is generated, then 2021 is listed before 2020 in one list.
4. **AC-04 — Prohibited behavior**: Given an unpublished entry or work, when the CV is generated, then no line is derived from it.
5. **AC-05 — Additional criterion**: Given a published group entry `Faltungen` at galerie burster, Karlsruhe, from 2025-12-01 to 2026-01-31, when the CV is generated in 2026, then its line reads `2025  Faltungen, galerie burster, Karlsruhe`.
